Good to know

Questions before you order

How do I place an order?

Send your event date, treat choice and quantity through the order form or WhatsApp. We will confirm availability, pricing and delivery or pickup details directly with you.

How far ahead should I book a cake?

Share your cake request as early as possible so there is enough time for your design and preparation. Exact availability is confirmed on WhatsApp.

Do you deliver?

Pickup and delivery arrangements are discussed for each order based on location and quantity. Use the booking form to start the conversation.

Can I mix items for an event?

Yes. Choose “A mix of treats” in the form and include the items you want in your request.

How is pricing confirmed?

Prices and quantities are confirmed after we understand your request. Send the size, quantity and occasion details so we can quote accurately.

Can I ask for a custom birthday surprise?

Yes. Describe the person, occasion and preferred items in the form. We will discuss available options with you on WhatsApp.
